## Pass notes

### Pass 1

The exact four passes and ascending pair order remain. The last live pool boundary is computed once because overlap separation changes positions only. The worker's same-turn replay comparison improved p95 from 7.934 to 6.598 ms; the host was slower than the frozen baseline, so the magnitude is indicative rather than cross-run release proof. The main-agent rerun independently matched replay, seed-1 product-loop, and dense golden objects.

### Pass 2

`separate` now reports strict overlap and the solver stops only after a whole ascending-order pass makes no separation. An eight-batch interleaved detached-head comparison showed replay p50 -5.9% and p95 -11.5%, but one candidate outlier regressed p99, so no tail improvement is claimed. Dense direction was favorable but the host was heavily contended. The main-agent rerun independently matched all three simulation goldens.

### Pass 3

An inline axis bound skips only pairs for which `dx^2 + dy^2 >= (ra + rb)^2` is guaranteed, while retaining the canonical ascending walk for every potentially overlapping pair. A 1,000,005-case finite oracle found no false rejection. Alternating replay batches favored the change, dense timing was too contended to interpret, and static grids/sweeps were rejected because earlier separations mutate later pair positions.

### Pass 4

`furthestClear` now branches once on x/y and feeds scalar candidate coordinates to the same 12 bisection predicates. Three 50-run dense e32/p0 batches improved median p95 from 305.119 to 147.298 ms, while the function's self samples fell from 11,155 to 3,994. The exact face/corner tangent path and all frozen simulation goldens remain unchanged.

### Pass 5

A two-bit `moveWithWalls` result prototype was rejected and reverted. Current profiling put direct CPU at 0.30%; low-interval heap attribution persisted even after explicit result objects were removed, and dense/replay timing directions conflicted. The required four-file representation change did not justify a final score of 1.0.

### Pass 6

An exact OOB-hoist/contiguous-index/radius-square prototype for `overlapsSolid` was correct but not faster: self samples changed by only -0.15%, and tail timing was variance-dominated. A 1,001,690-case boundary/fuzz oracle and all 827 tests passed before the prototype was reverted.

### Pass 7

Friendly shots now reject strict x/y misses before the unchanged `Math.hypot` circle test. Exact tangency, diagonals, NaNs, and near-boundary floats retain the canonical predicate; a 2,008,264-case differential oracle found zero mismatches. Dense 5x50 median p95 improved from 399.348 to 207.026 ms, with `updateProjectiles` self share falling from 43.23% to 15.18%.

### Pass 8

A zero-allocation 32-bit live-enemy mask retained ascending pool order and same-tick kill handling, but added work under full saturation. Dense p50/p95 regressed 2.78%/1.25% and `updateProjectiles` self share rose from 15.08% to 15.63%; the exact, fully tested prototype was reverted.

### Pass 9

Coalescing adjacent same-style BoltView fills cut the stationary synthetic dense-render p95 from 48.6 to 23.4 ms under SwiftShader, but 15 moving-bolt captures across three angles and five stages were not pixel-identical. Because visual isomorphism was unproven and the clean win was synthetic-only, the candidate was reverted at score 0.67.

### Pass 10

Fresh representative profiling showed `resolveOverlaps` at 3.78% versus 28.85% initially, so convergence moved to hostile projectile distance checks. Strict graze-square rejection preserves the canonical hypot path for every possible hit/graze, tangency, and non-finite input. A controlled hostile-projectile workload improved p50/p95 by 80.0%/87.7%; the main agent independently corrected the worker's seed mix-up and verified the frozen seed-1 product hash `407338761` plus replay/dense goldens.
