Descend

Death is the door.

A warrior falls on a nameless field and wakes in the bardo — the space between death and rebirth. Behind him, an empty suit of armor. Ahead, a threshold of gold.

The passage

  1. Fight
  2. Receive boons
  3. Descend
  4. Die or escape
  5. Return changed

Every swing is a commitment.

Weighted strikes, honest telegraphs, openings you earn. The bardo's wardens hit like falling architecture — and the fight never cheats. When you fall, you will know why.

No two underworlds ask the same price.

Each run crosses the death-realms of humanity's great faiths. Learn the weighing floor of Duat, the rime court of Niflheim, the wind of knives over Mictlan — each with its own rules, trials, and mercies.

What you rescue remains.

Souls freed and relics carried out of the dark settle in a town between worlds, rebuilt run by run from everything you refuse to leave behind. It remembers you — even when the underworlds don't.

The way out is through.
